# PT Besar Cipta Karya (BCKA) — Helper Mechanic Internship

**Role:** Helper Mechanic Intern  
**Location:** Workshop Mitra PAMA, Muara Tiga Besar Utara (MTBU)  
**Period:** 21 November 2024 – 21 February 2025  
**Internship type:** Praktik Industri Mandiri (PIM)

## Scope

This internship emphasized direct workshop activity and complemented the earlier maintenance-planning internship with hands-on maintenance exposure.

The official industry assessment identifies the internship section as **Helper Mekanik**.

## Documented Workshop Activities

### Scheduled Maintenance

- Assisted periodic service activities, including engine-oil and filter replacement.
- Assisted cooling-system inspection.
- Checked braking, transmission, tire, and electrical-system condition.
- Recorded equipment Hours Meter information.

### Corrective Maintenance

- Assisted troubleshooting and repair work involving:
  - hydraulic leaks,
  - engine overheating,
  - electrical-system faults.
- Assisted removal and reinstallation of damaged components under workshop supervision.

### Daily Inspection

- Performed visual checks before unit use, including:
  - oil condition/level,
  - radiator/cooling system,
  - brakes,
  - lamps.
- Assisted daily checklist documentation for breakdown and post-repair unit status.

### Parts & Warehouse Support

- Checked and arranged parts stock by type and unit.
- Helped locate, retrieve, and record components required by mechanics.
- Supported warehouse housekeeping and preparation of end-of-life parts for return handling.

### K3LH Support

- Assisted safety personnel with installation of safety signs.
- Assisted with placement of K3 equipment on units.
- Followed workshop safety procedures and use of personal protective equipment during internship activities.

## Other Workshop Exposure

The internship also included supporting activities such as scheduled unit cleaning and painting of repaired/end-of-service units. These are retained here as supporting exposure rather than presented as core maintenance competencies.

## Skills Reinforced

- Scheduled maintenance support
- Corrective maintenance support
- Mechanical component removal / installation
- Basic inspection of braking, transmission, cooling, tire, and electrical systems
- Daily inspection and maintenance documentation
- Parts and warehouse support
- Workshop teamwork
- K3LH awareness
