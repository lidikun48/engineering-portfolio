# PT Bukit Asam Tbk — Maintenance Planning Internship

**Area:** Perencanaan Perawatan Alat Produksi dan Penunjang Tambang  
**Location:** Mine Service Facility (MSF), Banko Barat, Tanjung Enim  
**Period:** 11 March 2024 – 11 June 2024  
**Internship type:** Praktik Industri Terbimbing (PIT)

## Scope

The internship focused on the workflow of maintenance planning for mining production and supporting equipment. The main documented activities were:

- Preparing **preventive maintenance schedules** based on operating-hour / Hours Meter records.
- Preparing **Work Orders**, task lists, PM check sheets, and supporting maintenance documents.
- Supporting **spare-part preparation** and availability checks using **Ellipse 9**.
- Monitoring maintenance progress through equipment inspection.
- Participating in commissioning activities for newly contracted equipment.
- Participating in lubricant-condition and contamination testing activities.

## Maintenance Planning Workflow

The internship provided exposure to the maintenance process from planning to work completion:

1. Collect and update equipment operating-hour data.
2. Build HM records and maintenance schedules.
3. Prepare Work Orders, task lists, Bill of Material, PM check sheets, and supporting documents.
4. Check and prepare required parts/material.
5. Coordinate the planned maintenance activity with operations and maintenance execution teams.
6. Monitor maintenance progress through inspection.
7. Receive maintenance realization reports and additional findings/backlogs.
8. Close the Work Order after the maintenance process is completed.

## Equipment & Activities Observed

Selected documented activities included:

- Preventive-maintenance inspection of **BelAZ 75135**.
- Commissioning of **Volvo FMX 420**.
- Commissioning of a **genset**.
- Commissioning of a **water-truck attachment**.
- Lubricant sampling and condition monitoring.

## Lubricant Condition Monitoring Exposure

The internship report documents exposure to several stages of lubricant evaluation:

- Visual inspection and sample preparation.
- TAN/TBN testing using **FluidScan**.
- Viscosity testing using **MiniVisc**.
- Contamination / wear-particle analysis using **LaserNet 200**.
- Elemental analysis using **Spectroil 100**.

These activities were part of maintenance-condition monitoring; this portfolio does not represent them as independent laboratory certification.

## Internship Case Study

**Preventive Maintenance Analysis — BelAZ 75135**

The academic report used BelAZ 75135 as the main case study to examine preventive-maintenance planning, HM-based scheduling, maintenance records, spare-part preparation, and maintenance realization.
